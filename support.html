Grown-ups settings

On the home screen, select ⚙️ and answer the grown-ups question. There you can see your subscription, restore purchases, sign out or delete your account.

Restore your subscription

Open ⚙️ Grown-ups and choose Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with. With Family Sharing, family members get the subscription too.

Cancel the subscription

Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions › Phonics Pals Plus. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or current year ends to avoid being charged.

Delete your account

Open ⚙️ Grown-ups, then Delete account, and confirm with Apple. This erases your child's progress from the device and your iCloud. To also disconnect Sign in with Apple, go to Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple › Phonics Pals › Stop Using. Deleting your account doesn't cancel the subscription.
