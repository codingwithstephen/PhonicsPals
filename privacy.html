Privacy Policy

Effective 12 October 2026 · Phonics Pals is published by Stephen Mooney (“we”, “us”).

Phonics Pals is a phonics app for children aged 4–7 on Apple TV, iPad and iPhone. We built it to collect as little as possible. We don't collect any personal information from children, we don't show ads, and we don't use analytics or tracking.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your Sign in with Apple account. When a parent signs in, Apple gives the app an anonymous account identifier and, the first time only, the name and email you choose to share. You can hide your email with Apple's “Hide My Email”. These details are stored on your device and are not sent to us.
  • Your child's progress (stars earned, sounds heard, words blended). This is stored on your device and synced between your family's devices through your own iCloud account (iCloud key-value storage). It is linked to your anonymous Apple account identifier. We can't access your iCloud data.
  • Subscriptions. Purchases, the free trial and renewals are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details. The App Store tells the app whether a subscription is active.

What we receive

Nothing. Phonics Pals has no server of its own and sends no data to us or to anyone else. All speech is recorded audio inside the app.

Children's privacy

Children never type names, emails or any other personal information into Phonics Pals. Signing in, purchases and settings sit behind a parental gate. We comply with the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the UK Age Appropriate Design Code by not collecting personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, open ⚙️ Grown-ups, then Delete account. This erases your child's progress from the device and from your iCloud and signs you out. To also disconnect Phonics Pals from your Apple Account, go to Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple › Phonics Pals › Stop Using.
  • Stop using Sign in with Apple: Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple › Phonics Pals.
  • Cancel the subscription: Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the effective date. If we ever wanted to collect more data, we'd ask first.
